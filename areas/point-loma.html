Real Estate Appraiser in Point Loma, California

Point Loma is a peninsula community bounded by San Diego Bay and the Pacific Ocean, combining historic bayside neighborhoods, ocean-bluff streets, and the revitalized Liberty Station district. Its distinct pockets and strong sense of place make it one of San Diego's most established coastal areas.

Point Loma Real Estate Market

The peninsula supports a range of price points, from modest cottages to premier bayfront and ocean-bluff estates. La Playa and the bayside carry the highest values, while Sunset Cliffs commands premiums for ocean views and bluff proximity, and Roseville and Loma Portal offer more established housing. Liberty Station has added newer attached and mixed-use product to the market. View orientation, whether toward the bay or the ocean, is a defining value driver across the peninsula. Careful identification of the specific neighborhood pocket is essential given the range of values.

Whether you're settling an estate, working through a divorce, establishing a date-of-death value, or planning a purchase or sale, a certified independent appraisal gives you a defensible opinion of value for property in Point Loma.

Local Highlights

Landmarks include Cabrillo National Monument, Sunset Cliffs Natural Park, Liberty Station, Shelter Island, and Point Loma High and Cabrillo elementary schools.

Local Valuation Considerations

Appraising Point Loma requires precise identification of the neighborhood pocket and the direction and quality of the view, since bay-facing La Playa and ocean-facing Sunset Cliffs behave as distinct submarkets. Bluff proximity, elevation, and historic character all factor into comparable selection.
