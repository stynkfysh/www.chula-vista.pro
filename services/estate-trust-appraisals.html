Estate & Trust Appraisals in Chula Vista & San Diego County

Estate and trust appraisals fix the fair market value of real property for probate administration, trust distribution, and tax reporting. Executors, trustees, and estate attorneys rely on a credible, independent valuation to administer assets and to treat every beneficiary evenhandedly.

Probate and Trust Administration

Whether a property is moving through probate or being distributed out of a trust, an impartial appraisal supports an equitable division and leaves a defensible record if any beneficiary later questions the number. I work regularly alongside San Diego County estate attorneys and professional fiduciaries.

Present-Day or Retrospective Values

Estate work often needs value as of the date of death or another specific date. I prepare both current and retrospective valuations, rebuilding the market as it existed on the date the assignment requires.

Reports That Survive Review

Every report is USPAP-compliant and arranged so attorneys, CPAs, the court, and beneficiaries can trace the analysis to its conclusion. Strong comparable support keeps disputes to a minimum and keeps administration on schedule.
